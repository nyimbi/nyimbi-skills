Which AI image model renders text best

Text in an image was the classic failure of image models and is now the clearest way to tell them apart. We asked 36 of them for the same poster: a title, a date line and a footer, in a specified style, and kept every result. The grid includes the September 18 additions; each model page dates its own run.

Anvisha Pai, Co-founder & CEO, Voyager

Verified 23 October 2026

What the side-by-side shows

The OpenAI models lead: GPT Image 2.5 Flare and Sunburst and GPT Image 2 set all three lines correctly with real poster hierarchy. Nano Banana Pro is as good, with the boldest type on the roster, and Seedream 5.0 Pro and Grok Imagine Pro are clean. FLUX.2 Max and Pro spell correctly but with a slightly softer finish. The failures: Seedream 4.5 broke the layout and dropped a stray 'D', Recraft V3 printed '@pm', Recraft V4.1 turned the words into unreadable shapes, Ideogram 4.0 left most of the poster empty, FLUX.2 Flex overlapped its lines, and Grok Imagine 1.0 let the words collide. Of the two open-weight additions, Qwen-Image set the poster cleanly and HiDream-I1 overlapped its title lines. FLUX 3 Image, added in October, set all three lines correctly in a two-colour layout with a clear hierarchy. Ideogram 4.5, added on 22 October, set all three lines correctly at every quality tier, with an extra 'LONDON' tag it was not asked for. Of the nine models added on 22 October, Krea 3, FLUX 3 Image Klein, Seedream 5.0 Flash, Muse Image, Bria FIBO 2.0, MOSS-Image and Recraft V4.1 Flash all set the three lines correctly with nothing added; both MAI-Image-2.5 models spelled them right but set the smaller lines in capitals. Ideogram Vector preserves the requested wording but adds an unwanted SUD IMERA line above it.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI image generator is best for text?

On a three-line poster, GPT Image 2.5, GPT Image 2, Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro and Grok Imagine Pro all set every word correctly. Ideogram, which markets text rendering, left most of the poster blank on this prompt.

Can Midjourney render text?

Midjourney is not on this roster. Of the models here, the OpenAI and Google models were the most reliable with words.
