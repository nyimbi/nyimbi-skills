Prefer not to decide the right model yourself?

Use a Model Router to automatically route each request to the best available model based on your cost, latency, or quality preferences. No additional cost.

Seedance 2.5 draft mode

Section titled “Seedance 2.5 draft mode”

Seedance 2.5 (seedance2_5) can render a faster 480p preview, then enhance that preview to 1080p. Use a preview to check a prompt, then pay the 1080p rate for the take you keep. See pricing for both rates.

Render a preview

Section titled “Render a preview”

Set draft to true on a normal text to video, image to video, or video to video request. Prompt, ratio, duration (including auto), references, audio, and seed work the same way as a full generation.

The preview is always 480p .mp4, and it is billed at the 480p rate. outputFormat: "mov" is rejected with draft: true, and the enhanced 1080p take is .mp4 too. ratio still chooses the aspect ratio: a 720p or 1080p ratio is accepted, and the preview uses the 480p frame for that aspect (16:9 / 9:16 land on 854:480 / 480:854).

Poll the returned task until status is SUCCEEDED. The task id is what you pass to enhance it.

Enhance a preview to 1080p

Section titled “Enhance a preview to 1080p”

Enhancement is always a text to video request, including when the preview came from image to video or video to video. Send model and draftTaskId only. The 1080p video reuses the preview’s prompt, references, duration, seed, and audio setting — when the preview used duration: "auto", the enhancement uses the measured length. An enhance request that also includes promptText, ratio, duration, seed, audio, references, or draft is rejected.

The preview task has to belong to the same account, have succeeded, and have been created with draft: true. Previews can be enhanced within 7 days of creation. The result is 1080p at the preview’s aspect ratio, billed at the 1080p rate for that output duration and any input video. The preview’s charge is unchanged.

Seedance 2.5 video to video modes

Section titled “Seedance 2.5 video to video modes”

On video to video, mode sets how Seedance 2.5 uses promptVideo:

modeWhat it doesRequirements
reference (default)Generates a new video conditioned on the input videoAccepts duration and ratio
extendContinues the input videoRequires promptText; does not accept ratio
editChanges the input video in placeRequires promptText and duration: "auto"; does not accept ratio

extend and edit keep the input video’s aspect ratio. They render at 1080p when the input video is larger than 720p, and at 720p otherwise.
