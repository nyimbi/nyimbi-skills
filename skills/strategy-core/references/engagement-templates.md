# Engagement Templates

## Decide

**Intake:** decision question, owner, deadline, stakes, options already on the table,
constraints, what happens if no decision is made.

**Artifacts:** `00-brief.md`, `03-options.md`, `04-model/`, `05-decision-memo.md`.

**Gate:** three distinct options; criteria agreed before scoring; model reproduces; kill
conditions named.

## Diagnose

**Intake:** symptom, when it started, what changed, what has been tried, available data.

**Artifacts:** `01-issue-tree.md`, `02-evidence.csv`, findings section of the memo.

**Gate:** each branch is MECE; each finding is labelled; the binding constraint is named, not
a list of contributors.

## Design

**Intake:** strategy already chosen, capabilities, current org, decision rights, constraints.

**Artifacts:** operating-model canvas, org and decision-rights map, capability plan,
transition sequence.

**Gate:** every capability has an owner; every decision has one accountable role; the design
fits the team's actual size.

## Plan

**Intake:** chosen direction, deadline, resources, dependencies.

**Artifacts:** 100-day or 18-month plan, OKRs, milestone dates, risk register, kill list.

**Gate:** each workstream has one owner; dependencies are explicit; the first milestone is
within two weeks.

## Review

**Intake:** the strategy or case to attack, its evidence, the decision it supports.

**Artifacts:** red-team memo: strongest objection, three weakest assumptions, cheapest tests,
what would change the answer.

**Gate:** every objection cites evidence or a missing test; no generic "execution risk".

## Present

**Intake:** the approved memo or deck-map, audience, decision the deck must win, venue and
time, format (`.pptx` or browser), template or brand assets, appendix material.

**Artifacts:** ghost deck (titles, exhibit sketches, data gaps), `.pptx` or open-slide
project, speaker notes, Q&A prep, appendix list.

**Gate:** ghost deck approved before design; one claim per slide; every number traces to the
memo or evidence ledger; 10–15 core slides; the ask is explicit; every slide rendered and
inspected before delivery. Method:
[`presentation-authoring/references/deck-craft.md`](../../presentation-authoring/references/deck-craft.md).

## Fundraise

**Intake:** stage, traction, market, model, round size, use of funds, investor profile.

**Artifacts:** narrative, financial model, deck, investor list, process plan, data room list.

**Gate:** every projection has a driver; TAM is cross-checked two ways; the ask and milestones
match the model.

## Negotiate

**Intake:** parties, interests, alternatives, deadlines, issues, tradeables.

**Artifacts:** BATNA/ZOPA calculation, issue matrix, trade plan, opening and walk-away.

**Gate:** BATNA is real and documented; ZOPA exists or the walk-away is recommended.

## Product

**Intake:** stage (idea, validated, live), customer, problem, current alternative, model,
goal.

**Artifacts:** opportunity brief, validation score, business model, persona/JTBD, positioning,
GTM, roadmap, experiment plan.

**Gate:** every score cites evidence; unit economics run; positioning survives a switch test;
launch has a kill criterion.

## 100-day

**Intake:** role or acquisition, mandate, stakeholders, first 30 days, what must not break.

**Artifacts:** day 1/30/100 plan, stakeholder map, listening tour, quick wins, decision log.

**Gate:** each 30-day block has one outcome; stakeholders are mapped by power and interest;
there is a "do not touch" list.
